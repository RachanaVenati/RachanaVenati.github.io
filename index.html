Experimental Evaluation of Axioms for Health Information Retrieval

Master’s thesis · From information retrieval and explainability to reranking and unit testing for retrieval systems

  • Designed and implemented seven explainable axioms covering medical terminology, age, sex, recency, and evidence level.
  • Built pairwise document-preference functions using MeSH-normalized medical concepts, PubMed metadata, and lexicon-based demographic matching.
  • Developed an axiomatic reranking pipeline using majority voting and KwikSort, with unit tests for preference functions and ranking behavior.
  • Performed post hoc and example-based analyses to compare axiom preferences with expert utility judgments and identify failures in neural retrieval rankings.
  • Evaluated the approach on top runs from TREC 2021 Health Misinformation and CLEF 2017 eHealth.
